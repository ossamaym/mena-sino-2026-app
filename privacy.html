MENA-SINO 2026 Programme — privacy

This app shows the scientific programme of the 11th MENA-SINO Congress, Luxor, 29–31 October 2026.

It does not create an account. It does not collect a name, email, or location. A starred personal plan is stored only on the phone. Industry logos are loaded from their public image addresses when the phone is online.

Contact: the MENA-SINO organising office, Alexandria University Stroke and Neurointervention Unit.
