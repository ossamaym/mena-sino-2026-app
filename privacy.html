MENA-SINO 2026 Programme — Privacy policy

Last updated 8 October 2026. Publisher: MENA-SINO / Alexandria University Stroke and Neurointervention Unit.

This app shows the congress programme for 29–31 October 2026 in Luxor. It does not create an account and does not send personal data to a server.

  • Programme data is stored in the app.
  • A starred personal plan is saved only on the device (local storage).
  • No location, contacts, camera, or advertising identifier is used.
  • Industry logos may load from the company sites named in the programme.

Contact: MENA-SINO congress secretariat, Alexandria University.
